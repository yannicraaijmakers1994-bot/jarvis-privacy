JARVIS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

JARVIS is a personal automation project that connects to the Instagram account authorized by its operator. This policy explains how information accessed through the Instagram API is handled.

Information processed

JARVIS may process information made available through the Instagram API for the connected account, including account identifiers, media information, comments, commenter usernames, and message or interaction data when the corresponding feature is enabled and authorized.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate JARVIS features requested by the account operator, such as monitoring Instagram content, processing comments, and preparing or sending replies. JARVIS does not sell personal information or use Instagram data for advertising.

Storage and security

Only information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and audit the automation may be stored. Access credentials and API tokens are treated as confidential and are not intentionally published.

Sharing

Information is not sold or shared with unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by service providers required to operate the authorized functionality, such as Meta/Instagram and the AI or infrastructure services configured by the operator.

Data deletion and access revocation

The Instagram connection can be revoked through the relevant Meta or Instagram account settings. Locally stored JARVIS Instagram data can also be deleted by the operator. Requests concerning data associated with this project may be submitted through the repository that hosts this policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated when JARVIS functionality changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an issue in the public JARVIS privacy repository. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
